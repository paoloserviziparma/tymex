Field service time tracking softwareService calls, technicians and hours under control.

Field service companies coordinate technicians, service calls, customers and jobs every day. With spreadsheets, emails or paper reports it's hard to know what was done and whether each call was profitable. Tymex records activities, hours and jobs simply and instantly.

The most common problems

  • service reports filled in late
  • hours worked not recorded properly
  • hard-to-control job costs
  • little visibility on service calls
  • too much admin work
  • out-of-date spreadsheets

These issues make cost control and planning harder.

How Tymex works

Technicians quickly log the details of each call. Every activity is linked to:

  • customer
  • job
  • activity
  • date
  • hours worked

Data is collected in real time and is immediately available to managers.

Frequently asked questions

What is field service software?+
It's a tool to manage service calls, activities, technicians and hours worked in one place.
Is Tymex suitable for technicians working off-site?+
Yes. The app is used directly on a smartphone during visits to customers.
Does Tymex replace paper service reports?+
Yes. Activities are recorded digitally, cutting errors and admin time.
Is Tymex useful for maintenance companies?+
Yes. It lets you monitor service calls, hours worked and jobs in a simple, structured way.
Can I check the margin on each service call?+
Yes. The data collected helps you assess the costs, time and profitability of the work done.
