Timesheet software for agenciesWhich clients really make you money?

Advertising and marketing agencies work on dozens of clients, campaigns and projects at once. Without time tracking it's hard to see which activities pay and which take more resources than planned. With Tymex, account managers, creatives, designers, consultants and project managers log the time they spend on each client.

Why agencies lose margin

  • work billed per project, fixed fee, retainer or hour package
  • unlogged hours stay invisible
  • extra work isn't counted
  • little visibility on projects

Tracking hours lets you compare time spent, budget and revenue objectively.

Frequently asked questions

Why should an agency track hours?+
Because many agencies bill per project, fixed fee, retainer or hour package: without precise data you can't tell whether a client is really profitable.
Can I see time per client and per activity?+
Yes. Each entry is linked to client, project and activity, and the dashboards show where time goes.
Is it useful for fixed-fee contracts?+
Yes. Comparing hours spent with the contract value shows whether the fee is sustainable.
