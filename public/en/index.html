FAQ

The answers you're looking for.

What is Tymex?+
Tymex is timesheet software for tracking hours worked on jobs and projects. Technicians, employees and contractors log activities, hours, customers and jobs from a smartphone or the web app; the company monitors costs, progress and margins without spreadsheets.
How long does it take to get started?+
Tymex is activated within 3 days of your order. After that, you simply log in from a browser to create users and give them access via app or web.
Can technicians in the field use it?+
Yes. Technicians log hours straight from their smartphone, even while working at customer sites.
Do I need an ERP to use it?+
No. Tymex works stand-alone on web and app. If you use eSOLVER by Sistemi Spa, you can connect it to sync customers, jobs, staff and cost-accounting entries.
Which devices does it run on?+
On iPhone and Android smartphones via the app, and on any PC via the web app at web.tymex.it.
How do you calculate a job's margin?+
By comparing a job's revenue with its costs. Tracking hours accurately shows which activities make a profit and which erode it.
