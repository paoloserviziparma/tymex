GlossaryTimesheet and job management glossary.

Definitions of the key terms in tracking hours worked, managing jobs and controlling company costs.

Timesheet
A timesheet is a tool used to record hours worked on activities, customers, projects and jobs. It tracks the time spent by staff and supports cost control.
Time tracking
Time tracking is the process of recording the hours worked by employees, technicians or contractors. It is essential for job control and for measuring productivity.
Job
A job is a set of activities carried out for a specific customer or project. Each job can have its own time, costs and financial targets to monitor.
Job management
Job management covers everything needed to plan, monitor and control the time, costs and financial results of a project or service call.
Actual reporting
Actual reporting means collecting and analysing the activities and hours actually carried out, in order to compare them with what was planned.
Profit margin
The profit margin is the difference between a job's revenue and its costs. It is one of the main indicators of a project's profitability.
Profitability
Profitability measures the ability of a job, a customer or an activity to generate profit for the company.
Hourly cost
The hourly cost is what one hour of a person's work costs the company, including salary, social security contributions and overheads.
Cost centre
A cost centre is an organisational unit used to allocate and monitor costs relating to departments, activities or projects.
Activity
An activity is a single task carried out by a person within a job, project or service call.
Customer
The customer is the party on whose behalf a job, project or service call is carried out.
Project
A project is an organised set of activities aimed at a specific goal within a defined time and budget.
Service call
A service call is an activity carried out at a customer's site for installation, maintenance, support or specialist assistance.
Service report
The service report is the document describing the work done, the time spent and any materials used during a service call.
Field technician
A field technician works at customer sites or construction sites and records activities, times and service calls directly from the workplace.
Timesheet app
A timesheet app is a mobile application for recording hours worked, activities and jobs from a smartphone or tablet.
Dashboard
A dashboard is a screen that gathers indicators and data to monitor activities, costs, hours worked and job progress.
KPI
KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) are indicators used to measure company performance and assess operational efficiency.
Management control
Management control is the set of activities used to monitor a company's costs, revenue and financial results.
Resource planning
Resource planning means assigning people, time and activities to the various jobs or projects.
ERP
An ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system is business software that integrates the main company processes, such as administration, sales, purchasing and production.
eSOLVER
eSOLVER is an ERP system used by many Italian companies for administrative, commercial and production management.
ERP integration
ERP integration lets timesheet software and the company's ERP exchange data, avoiding manual entry and duplicates.
API
APIs (Application Programming Interfaces) let different software applications communicate and exchange information automatically.
Ticket
A ticket is a request for assistance, maintenance or support that creates an activity to be managed and monitored.
SLA
An SLA (Service Level Agreement) defines the service levels agreed between supplier and customer, including response and intervention times.
Workflow
A workflow is an organised sequence of activities describing how a business process is handled.
Resource
A resource is a person, a team or another company asset involved in carrying out activities and jobs.
Operational efficiency
Operational efficiency measures the ability to carry out activities making the best use of time, resources and costs.
Business intelligence
Business intelligence covers the tools and methods that turn company data into information useful for strategic decisions.
Attendance sheet
A document used to record employees' attendance hours during the working day.
Hours reporting
The process of collecting and presenting the hours worked for customers, jobs or projects.
Digital timesheet
A software system for recording and monitoring hours worked without paper documents or spreadsheets.
Activity management
The process of planning, assigning and monitoring the activities carried out by a company's staff.
Timesheet software
An application used to record hours worked, activities, customers and jobs in a central, controlled way.
