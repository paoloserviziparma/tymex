Following consultation of the site indicated above, data relating to identified or identifiable natural persons may be processed. The data controller is Servizi Informatici Srl, with registered office in via Sicuri 42 / a, Parma (PR), 43124, telephone 0521 252824, e-mail info@tymex.it

Legal basis of the processing

The personal data indicated on this page are processed as necessary for the pursuit of the legitimate interest of the owner in guaranteeing the regular functioning of the web services offered, in obtaining statistical information on visits and on the use of the services themselves (Article 6, paragraph 1 , letter f) of the Regulation) and as necessary for the execution of a contract or pre-contractual measures (Article 6, paragraph 1, letter b) of the Regulation) in order to respond to requests sent to us.

Types of data processed and purpose of the treatment

Navigation data

The computer systems and software procedures used to operate this site acquire, during their normal operation, some personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols. This category of data includes IP addresses or domain names of the computers and terminals used by users, the URI / URL (Uniform Resource Identifier / Locator) addresses of the requested resources, the time of the request, the method used in submit the request to the server, the size of the file obtained in response, the numerical code indicating the status of the response given by the server (successful, error, etc.), some information regarding the geolocation of the request and other parameters relating to the operating system and to the user's computer environment (fingerprint). This is information that is not collected with the aim of being associated with identified interested parties, but which by their very nature could allow users to be identified. These data, necessary for the use of web services, are also processed for the purpose of: obtaining statistical information on the use of the services (most visited pages, number of visitors per hour or day, geographical areas of origin, etc.); check the correct functioning of the services offered. The data (log-files, traffic data) could be used to ascertain responsibility in case of hypothetical computer crimes against the site, and for the same reason kept by the ISP (Internet Service Provider, Internet connectivity provider) and from the web host.

Data communicated by the user

The optional, explicit and voluntary sending of messages to the contact addresses indicated on the site entails the acquisition of the sender's contact data (e-mail address) necessary to reply, as well as all personal data included in the communications. The processing in question does not require the acquisition of consent since it is necessary for the execution of a contract or pre-contractual measures at the request of the interested party in order to respond to requests received by us; therefore the provision of data is to be considered necessary for the completion of the request: failure to provide them would make it impossible to follow up on the request received. The sending of messages does not involve the automatic subscription to a mailing list with a promotional or commercial purpose. The data collected in this way are kept for the time strictly necessary to respond to messages. In the event that a commercial relationship is established, the data will be kept at least for the duration of the relationship itself.

Contact form

Filling out the form in the "Contacts" section with the aim of requesting information, involves the acquisition of the sender's contact details (e-mail address) necessary to reply, as well as all personal data included in the communications. The treatment in question does not require the acquisition of consent since it is necessary for the execution of a contract or pre-contractual measures at the request of the interested party in order to respond to requests and commercial contacts received; therefore the provision of data is to be considered necessary for the completion of the request: failure to provide them would make it impossible to follow up on the request received. The compilation of the form does not involve the automatic subscription to a mailing list with a promotional or commercial purpose. The data collected in this way are kept for the time strictly necessary to respond to messages. In the event that a commercial relationship is established, the data will be kept at least for the duration of the relationship itself.

Recipients of the data

The navigation data are stored by the ISP (Internet Service Provider) and by the web host (subject that hosts the web server on their own physical machines) which keep them according to the times provided for by the law. The personal data collected following receipt of messages, contacts via the form are also processed by the internal staff authorized by the Data Controller, who acts on the basis of specific instructions provided regarding the purposes and methods of the processing itself. No profiling is carried out and the collected data are not subject to automated decision-making processes. The site is hosted on Netlify (Netlify, Inc., United States), which processes data as a data processor for hosting the site, storing navigation data (server logs) and receiving requests sent through the contact form (Netlify Forms). Visit statistics are produced by Netlify Analytics from the server logs, in aggregate form and without cookies. The web servers can be located both within and outside the European Union (for example the United States); data transfers outside the European Union are carried out guaranteeing an adequate level of protection according to the European Commission, in compliance with the Standard Contractual Clauses. External services

Buttons and links to the App Store, Google Play, YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook, Google Maps and the web app web.tymex.it open third-party services, which are subject to their own privacy notices. Videos on the site are loaded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) only after the user clicks the preview. The buttons for sharing articles on Facebook and LinkedIn are plain links: these services receive data only if the user clicks them.

Rights of the interested parties and right of complaint

Interested parties have the right to obtain from the Data Controller, in the cases provided for, access to their personal data and the correction or cancellation of the same or the limitation of the processing that concerns them or to oppose the processing (articles 15 to 22. of the Regulation). Interested parties who believe that the processing of personal data referring to them carried out through this site is in violation of the provisions of the Regulation have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (for example the Guarantor for the protection of personal data), as required by art. 77 of the Regulation itself, or to take the appropriate judicial offices (Article 79 of the Regulation). Requests relating to the aforementioned articles must be sent to the Data Controller using the contacts indicated at the top of this information document.
