What are cookies?

Cookies are small text strings that a website can send and install, while browsing, on the user's device, in order to store some information about the user; this information will then be retransmitted to the web server that hosts the site on subsequent accesses.

What are cookies used for?

Cookies are used for different purposes: to perform computer authentication (for example to access the personal account), monitor sessions and preserve user settings when switching from one web page to another (remembering for example the language of visualization of the site, storage of information on specific configurations regarding users who access the web server, storage of browsing preferences, collection of anonymous statistical data (for example to monitor access to the site), marketing.

Cookies can first be divided into two broad categories, concerning the subjective profile: first-party cookies, installed by the operator of the site the user is visiting and third-party cookies, installed by different services (for example Google, Facebook, etc. …).

Regarding the time profile, on the other hand, we have session cookies, which are automatically deleted when the browser is closed; persistent cookies, which instead remain until the pre-established expiration date.

Finally, cookies can be divided into technical or necessary cookies, necessary for browsing the website in order to guarantee the service, for example used to perform authentication, remember in which language the site is viewed, etc ...; analytics or statistical cookies, used by website managers to collect information on the number of users and how they visit the site, and then process statistics on the service and its use; profiling or marketing cookies used to lead back to specific, identified or identifiable subjects, specific actions or behavioral patterns recurring in the use of the features offered by the service (pattern) in order to create profiles to be grouped into homogeneous clusters, so that it is possible send targeted advertising messages, i.e. in line with the preferences expressed by the user in the context of surfing the net.

For the purpose of their installation and use, technical cookies do not require the user's consent, and are therefore also indicated as necessary, while profiling cookies, both first and third-party, can only be used with the consent of the of the user; finally, analytics or statistical cookies are compared to technical cookies only if they collect anonymous data, otherwise they are also subject to the user's prior consent.

What kind of cookies does this site use?

This site does not use cookies, whether technical, statistical or profiling, and does not use any other tracking tools. For this reason no consent banner is shown.

Visit statistics are obtained with Netlify Analytics, which processes the server logs in aggregate form (see the privacy policy) without installing anything on the user's device. External services: videos are loaded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) only if the user clicks the preview; from that moment YouTube may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Links to the App Store, Google Play, LinkedIn, Facebook, Google Maps and the web app web.tymex.it lead to third-party services, which are subject to their own cookie policies.

Deactivation of cookies on the browser

Each user can configure their browser not to automatically accept cookies. It is possible to delete all cookies installed in the browser or block the installation of new ones. To obtain specific instructions for each type of browser, select one of the following links:

  • SAFARI: https://support.apple.com/kb/PH19214?viewlocale=it_IT&locale=en_US
  • CHROME: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416?hl=it
  • FIREFOX: https://support.mozilla.org/it/kb/Attivare%20e%20disattivare%20i%20cookie
  • OPERA: http://help.opera.com/Windows/10.00/it/cookies.html

Through the browser settings it is then possible to delete cookies and navigation data (each browser has its own mode, so it is necessary to follow the appropriate guides on the web). It is good to remember that this operation could delete other data saved in the browser and also referring to other sites (for example passwords) and it is therefore necessary to select only the functions for deleting cookies and navigation data (for example the history and memory browser cache).

Finally, it is possible to activate the “Do Not Track” option, present in most of the latest generation browsers. Websites designed to respect this option, when activated, should automatically stop collecting some of your browsing data. As mentioned, however, not all websites are set up to respect this option.

However, we do not recommend the total disabling of cookies since it could make it impossible to navigate, and it is advisable to choose the setting to disable only the tracking elements.
