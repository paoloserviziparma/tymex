Timesheet software for design firmsDo you know what each project really costs you?

In design firms, time is the main resource. Projects, revisions, client meetings and technical work all need to be tracked accurately. With Tymex, designers, architects and engineers log the hours spent on each project simply and consistently.

Typical problems in design firms

  • poor visibility on costs
  • hard-to-control projects
  • reporting errors
  • no way to assess project profitability

Many firms still use spreadsheets or manual records to track hours worked.

Budgeted versus actual hours

The most common problem is the gap between the time estimated in the quote and the time actually spent. With Tymex you can compare:

  • budgeted hours
  • actual hours
  • estimated costs
  • actual costs

So you spot overruns immediately and make future quotes more accurate.

Frequently asked questions

Why should a design firm track hours?+
Because the time of architects, engineers and staff is the main cost: without accurate tracking you can't know what projects really cost.
Can I log hours by design phase?+
Yes. Each person links their hours to client, project, phase and activity.
Does Tymex help make quotes more accurate?+
Yes. Comparing budgeted and actual hours shows the gaps and helps you estimate future projects better.
